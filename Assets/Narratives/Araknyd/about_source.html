About Us

Araknyd builds the web the way nature intended: tangled, resilient, and slightly venomous.

Our Story

Araknyd started in a WeWork closet with one laptop, three energy drinks, and a domain we bought during a panic attack. Today we serve 47 customers (down from 48 after the Great CSV Incident) across fintech, healthtech, and "we're not sure yet" tech.

Our mission is simple: We Web Better™. Our legal team asked us to add "results may vary" but we ran out of Notion seats.
